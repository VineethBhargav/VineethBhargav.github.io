

## Why RVBC Realm?

I wanted a single place to organize everything I learn throughout my career and personal life.

Instead of keeping notes scattered across different applications, I decided to build my own knowledge website that I can continuously expand.

The goal is to create a digital knowledge repository (a "second brain") that grows with me over time.

---

## Objectives

- Build a personal knowledge website.
- Learn how modern documentation websites work.
- Document everything I learn.
- Create a reusable reference for future projects.
- Share knowledge with others.
